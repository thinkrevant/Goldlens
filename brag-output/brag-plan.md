# Brag Plan: GoldLens

## What is this app?
GoldLens puts MCX's four gold futures on one scale (₹ per 10 g of 999 gold), flags only the gaps that would pay after real costs, tests every idea honestly, and runs a lie detector on trading tips.

## The angle
An honest elevator pitch for a hackathon: "most backtests lie, we show the truth." The video proves it with the product's own numbers: four prices collapsing into one line, a ₹25.75 lakh paper profit shrinking to −₹3,493 once each mistake is removed, and a "40% guaranteed" tip getting stamped BUSTED.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Gold lens logo lands over four coloured price lines drawing across the frame, with the required title card: **"Hack in Hills, powered by TinyFish."** spoken and shown verbatim, then "Team TechFast presents GoldLens".

## Key moments (the middle)
- Four contract cards (GOLDM, GOLDTEN, GOLDGUINEA, GOLDPETAL) arrive one by one with their real quoted prices: ₹146,425 / ₹146,819 / ₹118,009 / ₹14,755.
- The real 12-month price chart morphs from four separate lines into one normalised line.
- Scanner cards with z-score gauges and the "edge vs cost hurdle" meter: quiet by design.
- The honesty counter: ₹25.75 lakh → −₹3,493 while the seven steps light up.
- Tip Checker: the real example tip types out, the rule appears, the BUSTED stamp slams, "Claimed 40% · Honest −1.7%".
- The Telegram morning brief builds line by line on a phone.

## Outro / punchline
"GoldLens. Honest intelligence for gold traders." Wordmark lockup, Team TechFast · Hack in Hills '26.

## User flow worth showing
Open the dashboard → see four prices on one scale → scanner says whether anything pays after costs → paste a tip → honest verdict. (Recreated from the real app components and real data.)

- Creative direction: confident hackathon elevator pitch, premium trading-terminal look
- Interpretation: calm, sure narration; snappy entrances with long, readable holds; one big moment (the counter) and one punchy one (the stamp).

## Share copy (draft)
Most trading backtests lie. GoldLens puts MCX's four gold futures on one scale, tests every idea after real costs, and even busts "guaranteed" tips. Built for Hack in Hills, powered by TinyFish.

## Audio direction
- Role: voice-led pitch with a warm, clean music bed underneath
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 (steady, clean; suits polished)
- Music treatment: fades in under the intro, ducked low (~0.12) while the voice speaks, lifts slightly at the outro and fades out
- Music cue guidance: preset at assets/music/cues/…vol-12….music-cues.json, tempo ~110 BPM; cues only cover 0-25 s, so lock at most one major moment (the intro logo/title or the counter start) to a strong cue; natural timing elsewhere because the voice sets the pace
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (the bed sits ducked under narration; reactive motion would mostly track the voice)
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched: soft drops for the four cards, one soft impact on the chart merge, a firm hit for the BUSTED stamp, a light glass ping for the Telegram message, a soft bell on the final lockup
- Restraint rule: no SFX over a spoken key number; nothing louder than the voice
